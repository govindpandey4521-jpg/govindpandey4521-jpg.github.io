What I did

  • Built document extraction using Python, pypdf and Textract, deployed on Lambda/S3.
  • Used an LLM to cross-check extracted values and adjust a confidence score based on text-matching agreement.
  • Added dynamic validation logic (e.g. dates can't post-date 2050) alongside other business rules.
  • Deployed and managed services with Docker across EC2, ECS and ECR.
  • Contributed to internal Databricks PoCs — Accelerators, AppKit, Replit semantic-layer, Genie.

Outcome

An automated, confidence-scored extraction pipeline that turns unstructured PDFs into structured, validated data flowing back into Salesforce.
